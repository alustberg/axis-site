The Shape of Who You Are

Brendan Marshall maps the fight for agency. But agency needs coordinates — and that's where the work starts.

Every so often, someone maps a frontier before it even has a name. Brendan Marshall just did.

His premise is simple: the last era captured our attention; the next will capture our choices. We risk letting AI decide for us until we've been optimized into a "cultivated species" — unless we build an economy designed to expand human agency instead.

Brendan argues that the fix is to hand interpretation back to the individual. But back to what, exactly? Agency requires coordinates.

That's where Axis comes in. You aren't a single coherence score; you are a distinct shape across four dimensions: Strategy, Systems, Humanity, and Authenticity. You can't return someone to themselves until you can show them that shape clearly enough to navigate by it under pressure.

Which brings us to the real game: when a machine reads who you are, does it answer to you, or to the system selling to you?

Read Brendan's piece. It's the clearest map of this new frontier I've seen. The real work starts where his map ends.
